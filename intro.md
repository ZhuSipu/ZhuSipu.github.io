## Research and Project Profile

Sipu's work focuses on the intersection of architecture, spatial intelligence, human-computer interaction, and AI in the built environment. His recent projects include:

1. Multisensory VR Study on Stress Recovery in Hospital Staff Break Spaces
Studies how visual, auditory, and olfactory stimuli jointly support stress recovery in hospital staff break spaces through VR-based experiments with psychological and physiological measures.

2. Human-Robot Interaction in Architectural Space
Examines how spatial scale influences human visual attention and perception toward robots through controlled eye-tracking experiments in corridor and hall settings.

3. AI-Assisted Multi-Scale Historic District Renewal
Investigates AI-assisted block-scale planning decision-making and deep learning-based courtyard generation for the renewal of Beijing's historic old city. The project is ongoing.

4. Evaluation of Aesthetic Perception in Large Language Models
Evaluates the aesthetic perception of large language models by comparing their judgments with human aesthetic responses.

5. Immersive Architectural Interaction Design with CAVE Projection
Explores immersive and interactive architectural design workflows using CAVE projection, VR simulation, and real-time digital environments.
